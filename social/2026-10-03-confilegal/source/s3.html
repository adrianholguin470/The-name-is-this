The operator's lesson

Build the defense before the inspection.

1

Write the interpretation down.

A dated internal memo stating which reading of the rule you adopted and why. Discrepancy becomes documented interpretive error, not concealment.

2

Make every transaction traceable.

Origin, destination, amount, record. If one of the 515 payments had been off the books, the same court would have seen hiding instead of a mistake.

3

Do it in the quiet weeks.

This is weeks of work, and almost nobody starts until the notice arrives. By then the file is evidence, not preparation.
