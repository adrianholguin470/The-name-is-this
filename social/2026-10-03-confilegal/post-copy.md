## LinkedIn (CannaShark page + Adrian's profile) — document carousel, 4 slides

Published in Madrid.

Confilegal, Spain's news site for the legal profession, ran my column last Friday. It started with one of their court stories: a watch company fined €133,251 for 515 cash payments of exactly €1,000, and a court that threw the fine out because the company's reading of the rule was a reasonable mistake.

What interested me was why it counted as reasonable. Every one of the 515 payments was on the books and could be followed from origin to destination. Nothing split, nothing hidden. Without that record, the same interpretation would have looked like concealment.

That is the work we do for cannabis operators in the United States, and it translates. Write down the interpretation you adopted and why, before anyone asks. Make every transaction traceable. Do it in the quiet weeks, because almost nobody starts until the notice arrives.

Thank you to the Confilegal team for running the column.
