“The most important protection for Missouri's newest cannabis entrepreneurs may not be another rule. It may be knowing when not to sign.

Watch for
Equity that vests on signature, not on delivery
A management agreement wearing a consulting label
Fees calculated against gross, not net
SOPs that do not describe your building
