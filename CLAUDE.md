# Project conventions

## Benchmark brands

- **Viral Coach (Daniel Iles)** is our reference brand for advertising, short-form content,
  and funnel design. Profile lives in `references/viral-coach.md`.
  - When proposing or evaluating any marketing, ad creative, offer, or funnel solution,
    check it against that profile and say explicitly where we are copying them and where we
    are deliberately diverging.
  - Keep the profile current: when new ad creatives, hooks, or funnel changes from them are
    found, append them to the reference file rather than describing them only in chat.

## Expert panel

When asked to run an "expert panel" or to weigh options from multiple viewpoints, always
include a **Viral Coach lens** seat alongside the other experts. That seat argues from the
playbook in `references/viral-coach.md`: hook-first, founder-led short-form, organic proof
before paid spend, free community as top of funnel, "see if you qualify" call CTA, and a
repeated one-line proof stack. Flag where that playbook would not fit our case.
