  1. Commentary Missouri Independent October 2, 2026

    Missouri tightened the rules. The wait still leaves microbusiness owners vulnerable

    Missouri's May 2026 microbusiness rules fixed the paperwork problem: they define majority ownership and operation, let regulators review ownership arrangements before a license issues, require a compliance course, and make the eligible owner the primary contact with the state. What no rule can remove is the gap between winning a license and opening, when a new licensee holds something valuable and has little experience defending it.

    • Missouri drew its final round of microbusiness applicants on September 9, 2026: 888 applications, roughly 77 licenses expected starting in December.
    • Eligible owners must now be the primary point of contact with regulators during the application, limiting how consultants make themselves indispensable.
    • The first-round winner who opened the state's first microbusiness dispensary needed about two years, not the six months he expected.
    • The most important protection for a new licensee is knowing when not to sign.

  2. Op-Ed Marijuana Moment September 10, 2026

    California Marijuana Businesses Should Act Now To Comply With New Packaging Rules That Don't Take Effect Until 2028

    AB 2249 draws a clearer line around what California cannabis packaging, labels and ads may look like, with the new definition taking effect January 1, 2028. Because artwork is locked with the printer months before a run and boxes then sit in a warehouse, the real decision point for operators lands in the next two quarters, not in 2028.

    • Governor Gavin Newsom signed AB 2249 on August 31, 2026. The new packaging definition takes effect January 1, 2028.
    • Prohibited: cartoon characters, anyone who looks under 21, candy or dessert imagery aimed at children, bubble lettering, and packaging that mimics kids' products.
    • Realistic fruit that reflects an actual ingredient or growing region remains allowed.
    • Artwork is typically locked weeks or months before a print run, so the compliance deadline arrives well before the legal one.
