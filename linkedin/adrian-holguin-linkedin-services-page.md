## 5. Inbound reply template (LinkedIn Services proposal requests)

LinkedIn lets you send one proposal per request. This is the reply for a
qualified cannabis request. Under 600 characters so it fits without a scroll.

```
Thanks for the request. From what you wrote, the first thing I would look at is [the specific issue they named]. I have handled this on the operator side in [state or facility type], so I can be useful quickly.

Two ways to start:
1. A 30-minute triage call, no charge, where I tell you what I would fix first and in what order.
2. If it is already urgent, a fixed two-month implementation engagement with no ongoing retainer.

Reply with a time that works and I will send a calendar link.
```

For a request outside cannabis, hemp, or plant-alkaloid (like Quickfill Rx),
route it to Holguin & Company and use the same template with the industry
line removed.

For requests with no fit (offshore, unrelated trades, "need a business plan
written by Friday"), decline in the LinkedIn flow and do not reply. Every
proposal you send counts toward your response-rate score, and ignoring them
does not.
