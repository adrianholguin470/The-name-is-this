The Product

The Diagnostic & 90-Day Roadmap

One review across seven lenses. Written findings, the constraint named, a prioritized 90-day roadmap you can execute immediately, with or without us.

A business performance diagnostic is a structured review of how a company actually runs, across every function at once, ending in the constraint named with evidence and a sequenced plan to remove it. Holguin & Company’s Diagnostic reviews seven lenses and delivers written findings and a prioritized 90-day roadmap. The Diagnostic scales with your company — $5,000 to $24,000+ — applied in full when we move to execution.

Why the whole business

Not the symptom.

Sales, operations, people, and cash affect one another. Improving one without understanding the others moves the problem instead of resolving it. Every business is unique, but the constraint is always findable, and it is rarely the thing the owner came to talk about.

The Seven Lenses

I

Financial Performance

Revenue and margins by line, pricing and unit economics, cash conversion, the three numbers the owner watches and the three they should.

II

Sales & Marketing

Lead generation, positioning, conversion and follow-up, the sales process as it is actually run.

III

Operations

Workflows and SOPs, efficiency and capacity, where work waits, who touches it twice.

IV

People & Accountability

Team structure, responsibilities, productivity, leadership, and how much of the business depends on the owner being present.

V

Strategy & Growth

Business model, priorities, expansion, capital allocation, growth planning.

VI

Systems & Technology

Reporting and dashboards, AI and automation, systems integration, the stack.

VII

Complex & Regulated Matters

Licensing and compliance, market entry, expansion, industry-specific challenges.
