Questions, Answered

Asked before every engagement.

What does the Diagnostic cost?+
The Diagnostic scales with your company — $5,000 to $24,000+ — applied in full when we move to execution. The specific tier is confirmed on the evaluation call, based on the scale and complexity of the organization.
What is the Business Evaluation Call?+
A focused 30-minute conversation at no charge — your business, your goals, and what is actually in the way. Its only purpose is to see whether there is a fit. No deck, no pitch.
What do I receive from the Diagnostic?+
Three things: the constraint actually holding the business back, named with evidence; a written findings brief that is yours to keep, whoever executes it; and a prioritized 90-day roadmap sequenced for your business — not a template.
What kinds of companies does Holguin & Company work with?+
Founder-led and owner-operated companies, with particular experience in complex and regulated matters. Engagements are accepted in limited concurrent numbers so each receives the principal's attention.
What happens after the Diagnostic?+
Execute & Grow — the engagement. It runs in one of three modes: your team implements the roadmap, Holguin & Company works alongside your team, or the firm leads and builds the capability. The Diagnostic investment is applied in full when we move to execution.

Holguin & Company is based in Beverly Hills, California, and works with companies across North America, Latin America, and Europe. Meetings are by appointment.
