## H1: Strategy execution consulting

**Answer first:** Strategy execution consulting is the work of turning a company's plan into a running system: named owners, dated commitments, a weekly review, and a scorecard the leadership team manages against. Holguin & Company provides this as a hands-on engagement, typically 90 days after a diagnostic, for owner-led companies in California and nationally.

## H2: The problem it solves

Most owner-led companies do not have a strategy problem. They have a Tuesday problem. The plan was clear in January. By March the team is back to reacting, the owner is the only one holding the whole picture, and the quarterly goals are a memory.

Execution fails for predictable reasons:

- Priorities are not ranked, so everything is urgent.
- Nobody owns the outcome, only the task.
- There is no fixed rhythm for reviewing numbers and making decisions.
- The owner changes direction faster than the team can absorb.
- Constraints in cash, capacity, or people are not named out loud.

## H2: What the engagement includes

**Roadmap lock.** We start from the 90-day roadmap produced by the diagnostic (or build one in the first two weeks if you are starting here). Every item gets one owner, one date, one measure.

**Weekly cadence.** A 60-minute leadership meeting we design and, for the first month, run. Scorecard review, blockers, decisions, commitments. Same agenda every week.

**Scorecard.** Eight to twelve numbers that tell you whether the business is on plan, updated weekly, visible to every leader.

**Decision rights.** Who decides what, written down, so the owner stops being the bottleneck.

**Operator coaching.** One-to-one time with each leader on their commitments, between meetings.

**Hand-off.** By week twelve your team runs the cadence. We step back to a monthly check-in or exit.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

**How is this different from a strategy consultant?**
A strategy consultant leaves you a plan. We stay and run it with you until the plan is a habit.

**How long does it take?**
Ninety days is the standard engagement after a diagnostic. Most teams are running the cadence on their own by week eight to twelve.

**Do you need a diagnostic first?**
It is strongly recommended. The diagnostic finds the real constraint, and executing against the wrong constraint is expensive. If you already have a clear roadmap, we can start here.
