## H2: Who this is for

You own or run a company that has outgrown the way it is managed. The strategy exists, usually in your head and a deck. What is missing is the discipline that turns it into decisions, owners, deadlines, and numbers the team reviews every week.

Typical situations:

- Growth has stalled and nobody can say exactly where the constraint is.
- Leaders are busy, but priorities change every time you walk in the room.
- You are planning a buildout, a new location, a new market, or an exit, and the operating side is not ready.
- The business runs on you, and you want it to run on a system.

## H2: What we do

**Business Performance Diagnostic.** A 30-day look at the whole operation: finance, workflow, management system, and people. You get a written findings report and a 90-day roadmap with owners and dates. → `/services/business-performance-diagnostic`

**Strategy Execution.** We stay on after the diagnostic to run the roadmap with your team: weekly cadence, scorecards, decision rights, and the uncomfortable conversations that keep it moving. → `/services/strategy-execution`

**Operating Cadence.** The meeting and metric rhythm that lets a leadership team manage the company without the owner in every room. Installed in about 90 days. → `/services/operating-cadence`
