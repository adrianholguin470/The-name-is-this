## H1: The Diagnostic & 90-Day Roadmap

**Answer first.** A business performance diagnostic is a structured review of how a company actually runs, across every function at once, ending in the constraint named with evidence and a sequenced plan to remove it. Holguin & Company's Diagnostic reviews seven lenses and delivers written findings and a prioritized 90-day roadmap. The Diagnostic scales with your company — $5,000 to $24,000+ — applied in full when we move to execution.

## H2: Why the whole business, not the symptom

Sales, operations, people, and cash affect one another. Improving one without understanding the others moves the problem instead of resolving it. Every business is unique, but the constraint is always findable, and it is rarely the thing the owner came to talk about.

## H2: The seven lenses

**I. Financial Performance.** Revenue and margins by line, pricing and unit economics, cash conversion, the three numbers the owner watches and the three they should.

**II. Sales & Marketing.** Lead generation, positioning, conversion and follow-up, the sales process as it is actually run.

**III. Operations.** Workflows and SOPs, efficiency and capacity, where work waits, who touches it twice.

**IV. People & Accountability.** Team structure, responsibilities, productivity, leadership, and how much of the business depends on the owner being present.

**V. Strategy & Growth.** Business model, priorities, expansion, capital allocation, growth planning.

**VI. Systems & Technology.** Reporting and dashboards, AI and automation, systems integration, the stack.

**VII. Complex & Regulated Matters.** Licensing and compliance, market entry, expansion, industry-specific challenges.

## H2: How it runs

1. **The Business Evaluation Call.** 30 minutes, no charge. Your business, your goals, what is actually in the way. Its only purpose is to see whether there is a fit.
2. **The Diagnostic.** Interviews with the owner and each leader, data review, process walk-throughs. [CONFIRM: duration, e.g. "three to four weeks".] Findings are written for the owner first.
3. **The roadmap.** Six to ten initiatives, each with an owner, a date, and a measure, sequenced for your business. Presented to the leadership team.

## H2: What you leave with

- **The constraint, named.** The one thing actually holding the business back, with the evidence.
- **A written findings brief.** Clear, specific, and yours to keep, whoever executes it.
- **A prioritized 90-day roadmap.** Sequenced, practical, built for your business, not a template.

## H2: Then: Execute & Grow

The roadmap can be run by your team, alongside Holguin & Company, or firm-led. The Diagnostic investment is applied in full when we move to execution.
