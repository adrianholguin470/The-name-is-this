## H1: Business performance diagnostic

**Answer first:** A business performance diagnostic is a structured 30-day review of how a company actually operates: its financial model, its core workflows, its management system, and its people. Holguin & Company delivers the findings as a written report and a 90-day roadmap with named owners and dates. It is the starting point for every execution engagement.

## H2: What we look at

**Finance.** Margin by product or service line, cash conversion, pricing discipline, the three numbers the owner watches and the three they should.

**Workflow.** How work moves from sale to delivery to cash. Where it waits. Who touches it twice.

**Management system.** How priorities get set, who decides, what gets reviewed and when. Usually the biggest gap.

**People.** Whether the leadership team is structured for the business you have now, and whether each seat has an owner who can own an outcome.

## H2: How it works

1. **Kickoff (week 1).** Agree scope, collect financials and operating data, schedule interviews.
2. **Discovery (weeks 1–3).** Interviews with the owner, each leader, and a cross-section of staff. Process walk-throughs. Data review.
3. **Findings (week 4).** Written report: what is working, the top constraints ranked, and what each one costs.
4. **Roadmap (week 4).** A 90-day plan, usually six to ten initiatives, each with an owner, a date, and a measure. Presented to the leadership team.

## H2: What you walk away with

- A findings report you can hand to a partner, a lender, or a buyer
- One ranked list of constraints, with the first one named
- A 90-day roadmap your team can run with or without us
- A clear recommendation on whether an execution engagement is worth it

## H2: Who should buy this

- Owners planning a buildout, new location, acquisition, or exit in the next 12 months
- Companies where growth has flattened and the cause is disputed
- Leadership teams taking over from a founder
- Operators in regulated industries preparing for a license, inspection, or investor diligence

## H2: Frequently asked questions

**How much of my team's time does it take?**
About one hour per leader for interviews, plus access to data. The owner spends three to four hours across the month.

**Is it confidential?**
Yes. Findings go to the owner first. Nothing is shared with the team until you decide what to share.

**What if the findings are uncomfortable?**
They usually are. The report is written to be acted on, not to flatter. That is what you are paying for.
