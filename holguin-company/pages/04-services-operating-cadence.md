## H1: Operating cadence for leadership teams

**Answer first:** An operating cadence is the fixed rhythm of meetings, metrics, and decisions a leadership team uses to run a company: a weekly scorecard review, a monthly financial and priorities review, and a quarterly planning session. Holguin & Company designs and installs this rhythm in about 90 days, so the business runs on a system instead of on the owner.

## H2: Why cadence matters more than the plan

A plan is a document. A cadence is a behavior. Companies that execute well are rarely smarter than the ones that do not; they review the same numbers on the same day every week and make decisions on the spot. The rhythm does the work.

Signs you do not have one:

- Meetings happen when something breaks.
- The same issues come up month after month without a decision.
- Leaders learn about priorities from the owner's mood.
- Financials arrive late and get discussed never.

## H2: What we install

| Rhythm | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Daily huddle (optional) | 10 min | Blockers only, for operating teams that need it |
| Weekly leadership meeting | 60 min | Scorecard, roadmap commitments, decisions |
| Monthly business review | 2 hrs | Financials, pipeline, people, next month's priorities |
| Quarterly planning | 1 day | Reset the 90-day roadmap against the annual plan |
| Annual planning | 2 days | Strategy, budget, structure |

For each: the agenda, the pre-read, the scorecard, who attends, who decides, and who writes it down.

## H2: How the engagement runs

1. **Weeks 1–2: Design.** Interview each leader, review the current meeting load (most teams meet too much and decide too little), and draft the cadence.
2. **Weeks 3–8: Run it with you.** We facilitate the weekly and the first monthly, then hand facilitation to a leader in your team.
3. **Weeks 9–12: Hand off.** We attend as observer, coach the facilitator, and leave a written operating manual.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

**Is this EOS or Scaling Up?**
It borrows from the same ideas (fixed rhythm, scorecard, ranked priorities) without a licensed system. We adapt to your business rather than fitting your business to a framework. [CONFIRM: your view on this]

**How many meetings will my team have?**
Usually fewer than today. Cadence replaces ad-hoc meetings; it does not add to them.

**Can we do this without the owner attending every meeting?**
That is the point. By the end, the owner attends the monthly and quarterly, and the weekly runs without them.

**What if my leaders are not ready for this?**
The cadence exposes that quickly, which is useful. Where someone cannot own an outcome, we help you see it and decide.
