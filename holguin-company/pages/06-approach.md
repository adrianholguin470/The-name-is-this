## H1: How we work

Every engagement follows the same three steps. The steps are fixed; the content is yours.

## H2: Step 1. Diagnostic call (30 minutes, no fee)

We talk about the business, what has stalled, and what you have already tried. We say plainly whether a diagnostic is the right next step. About [CONFIRM: a third?] of these calls end with a referral elsewhere or a "not yet".

## H2: Step 2. Business performance diagnostic (30 days, fixed fee)

Interviews, data, process walk-throughs. A written findings report and a 90-day roadmap with owners, dates, and measures. You can stop here and run the roadmap yourself. → `/services/business-performance-diagnostic`

## H2: Step 3. Execution (90 days, fixed fee)

We run the roadmap with your leadership team: weekly cadence, scorecard, decision rights, coaching. By the end, your team runs the system and we step back. → `/services/strategy-execution`

## H2: Principles we hold to

**One constraint at a time.** Every company has several problems and one constraint. We name it and work it first.

**Owners, dates, measures.** Nothing goes on a roadmap without all three.

**Fixed fee, defined exit.** You know the price before we start and the date we leave. We are not trying to become permanent.

**Say the hard thing.** Findings are written to be acted on. If the constraint is a person, including the owner, the report says so.

**Audit-grade discipline.** The firm's roots are in regulated industries, where a missed procedure can cost a license. We bring that standard to every client.

## H2: What we ask of you

- The owner attends the weekly for the first month and the monthly thereafter.
- Leaders own outcomes, not tasks, and report against them.
- Data is shared early and completely.
- Decisions get made in the meeting, not after it.

## H2: What we do not do

- Open-ended retainers with no deliverable
- Strategy decks without an execution plan
- Interim management (we build your team's ability to run the company, we do not run it for you) [CONFIRM: unless you offer embedded buildout under H&C as CannaShark does]
